Explanation of Water Usage Data

The bar chart compares water usage across Australian states and territories in 2022, focusing on two primary sectors: industry and households. The blue bars represent water consumption by industry, while the orange bars represent household water usage. Notably, states like New South Wales and Victoria exhibit higher water usage in both sectors, with industry consuming significantly more than households. In contrast, states like Tasmania and Northern Territory have relatively lower water consumption across both categories. This visual highlights the varying water demands of different regions, offering insight into how water resources are distributed between industrial and domestic needs.

Explanation of Water Usage Data

This line chart shows Australia's water expenditure from 2015 to 2022 across four categories: Households Distributed Water, Households Sewerage and Wastewater, Industry Distributed Water, and Industry Sewerage and Wastewater. Household distributed water had the highest costs, reaching 6,223 million AUD in 2022. Household sewerage and wastewater followed closely, rising to 5,778 million AUD. Industry distributed water saw moderate growth, peaking at 3,157 million AUD in 2019, while industry sewerage and wastewater remained the lowest, reaching 1,341 million AUD in 2022. Overall, household water costs were significantly higher than those for industries.
